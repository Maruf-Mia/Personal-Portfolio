About Me

Eight years ago I was a junior developer fixing bugs on a small team and learning how much of the job happens after the code is written: reading other people’s work, debugging production, and explaining trade-offs in plain language.

That curiosity turned into ownership. I moved from single features to whole services, then to designing systems and choosing what not to build. Along the way I learned that the fastest teams are the ones that write things down and review each other’s work kindly.

Today I lead engineers, mentor newer developers, and stay hands-on in the code. I’ve delivered 50+ projects, and I’m now looking for a fully remote team where I can keep growing and keep raising the bar.
